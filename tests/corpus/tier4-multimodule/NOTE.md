# Tier 4 — software-engineering-scale, multi-module

Multi-package / multi-module programs are a **Stage 2** capability (packages, the authority
lockfile, cross-package visibility). Stage 1 resolves a single module per file, so the
software-engineering-scale corpus lands with `STAGE2_SPECIFICATION.md`.

Until then, the single-file programs in `tier3-application/` exercise the largest programs the
Stage-1 compiler admits, and `tier5-security/` exercises the authority-critical scenarios.
